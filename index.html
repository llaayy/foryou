Chapter II

Things I never said properly.

02
written with a little courage

Hey, you.

I spent a lot of time thinking about us — not just the ending, but the reason I cared so much in the first place.

I miss your laugh. I miss the way an ordinary day could suddenly feel special because I got to share it with you. I miss the version of me that existed when things felt easy between us.

I know missing someone isn't the same thing as being ready to do everything right. So I'm not asking you to forget what happened. I'm asking you to remember that I did care, that I still care, and that I learned things from losing you.

Maybe there can be a new chapter.

Not the old us. A better one. Slower. Kinder. More honest.

And if you don't want that, I'll respect it. I just didn't want the last thing between us to be silence.

— me ♡
